An Interactive Eco Ecosystem

Explore games, simulators, and calculators designed to make environmental education highly engaging and action-oriented.

Learning Syllabus

8 comprehensive modules covering Climate Change, Pollution, Waste, Renewables, and Deforestation. Complete lessons to earn Eco Coins.

Interactive City Simulator

Construct clean power grids, recycling centers, and forest reserves. Watch live air quality and citizen happiness scores recalculate.

Virtual Canvas Garden

Buy seeds using your earned coins and nurture them from seed to sprout. Water, fertilize, and grow a diverse forest ecosystem.

Sorting & Segregation Game

A fast-paced drag-and-drop game. Sort plastic containers, batteries, and organic peels into correct bins under time limits.

Carbon Footprint Calculator

Measure your personal and household carbon footprint across transport, energy, and diet. Get personalised eco-reduction tips.

Quiz Arena

Challenge yourself with topic-specific quizzes across all 8 eco modules. Earn XP, climb the leaderboard, and unlock achievement badges.
